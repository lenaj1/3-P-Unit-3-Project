Butter Mochi Recepie

  • Time: 1hr 30min
  • Serves: 20
]
Ingredients Quantity
Unsalted butter or vegetable oil For coating the pan
Unsalted butter 8 tbsp (1 stick)
Mochiko 1 Pound
Granulated Sugar 2 Cups
Baking Powder 2 Teaspoons
Milk 2 Cups
Salt 1/2 Teaspoons
Eggs 4 Large
Vanilla Extract 2 Teaspoons
Unsweetened Coconut Milk 13.5 Ounce (1 Can)
Shredded Coconut 1/2 Cup
Flaky Salt Pinch (Optional)

Step 1: Grease the Pan. Preheat the oven at 350 degrees F.


Step 2: Mix dry ingreditents, then mix wet ingredients in another bowl.


Step 3: Mix together wet and dry ingredients.


Step 4: Pour mixture into prepared pan.


Step 5 Sprinkle Coconut on top. (and salt if desired)


Step 6 Bake Until golden at the top (60-70 min)


Step 7 Let the Mochi cool for 1 hour. Cut and Enjoy!
